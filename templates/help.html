How AgriConnect works

Predict Price

Uses a Random Forest model trained on historical mandi (market) price records for six crops across Bengaluru and Mysuru districts. Choose a crop, district, market, variety, and grade, then compare a current-year price against any future year for the same month.

Recommend Crop

Uses separate time-series forecasts (Prophet or Holt-Winters, whichever performed better historically for that crop) covering 6 commodities. Crops are ranked by projected price growth over the next 90 days, scaled down for crops where the forecast has historically had a wider margin of error.

Things to keep in mind

  • Price predictions are estimates from historical patterns, not guarantees.
  • The crop recommendation is based on price trend only — there's no market demand or sown-area data behind it.
  • Predictions for years far beyond the historical data range are less reliable.
  • Not every crop/variety combination on the Predict Price form occurred in the original data.
